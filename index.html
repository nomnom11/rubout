About Rubout

Rubout is a twin-stick shooter drawn on graph paper. The enemies are numbers with arms and legs, and they have no health bars. The only way to remove one is to bring it to exactly zero with the operations on your toolbelt.

How a run works

  • Clear the page. Every page sends a group of numbers after you. Erase them all to finish it.
  • Pick a tool. Between pages you choose one new operation or one upgrade. The toolbelt holds up to 9 operations.
  • Stay alive. You have 3 lives, shown at the top. Touching a number costs one life and knocks nearby numbers back. Every 5th page gives one life back.
  • Go deeper. Later pages bring more numbers, bigger numbers and new kinds of enemies. The run ends when your last life is gone.

Meet the cast

You: ScribbleThe hero

A stickman with a highlighter-yellow head and a pencil for a gun. Scribble walks, aims and fires in any direction. A shot stops at the first number it hits, unless you have Sharp Lead.

Plain numbersFrom page 1

Divide while you can, then finish with − or +.

24 → ÷2 → 12 → ÷2 → 6 → ÷2 → 3 → −1 three times → 0
PrimesFrom page 2

A blue ring means only 1 and the number itself divide it, so ÷ bounces off. Subtract or add to change it into something divisible.

7 → −1 → 6 → ÷2 → 3 → −1 → 2 → ÷2 → 1 → −1 → 0
RunnersFrom page 3

Small numbers (2 to 8) that run as fast as you do, so you cannot outrun them. Shoot them early.

6 → ÷2 → 3 → −1 three times → 0
NegativesFrom page 4

Drawn in red. Subtracting pushes them deeper, so add instead. A ×−1 shot flips the sign so you can shoot the number down.

−3 → +1 three times → 0
FactorialsFrom page 6

n! means 1 × 2 × … × n, so 4! is 24. Each time the dashed ring fills, the number grows one step, up to 5! = 120. Any hit freezes it into a plain number.

4! → ÷2 → 12 → ÷2 → 6 → ÷2 → 3 → −1 three times → 0
Big numbersEvery 5th page

Slow, huge and marked in yellow. They are always a power of 2, so halving works. Touching one costs a life but does not erase it.

64 → ÷2 six times → 1 → −1 → 0

Your toolbelt

Every shot applies one operation to the number it hits. ÷ only works when the result is a whole number, and √ only works on perfect squares. A shot that does not work shows “no go”. Subtracting past zero is allowed, but the number turns negative. Yellow tiles are the three operations you start with.

Upgrades

  • Quick Hand. Fire 20% faster. Up to 3 levels.
  • Light Feet. Move 12% faster. Up to 3 levels.
  • Sharp Lead. Shots pass through one more number. Up to 3 levels.
  • Spare Eraser. Gain one life, up to a total of 5.

Scoring

Every erase scores 10 points plus the number's starting size, with the size bonus capped at 60. Erase numbers within about two seconds of each other to build a combo that multiplies your points, up to ×5. Taking a hit resets the combo.

Tips

  • Check divisibility first. Even numbers take ÷2, numbers whose digits add up to a multiple of 3 take ÷3, and numbers ending in 0 or 5 take ÷5.
  • Dividing is the fast way down. Subtracting and adding finish the job.
  • Enemies sway as they walk. Keep moving in wide circles and shoot across their path.
  • A hit knocks nearby numbers away. Treat that breathing room as a last resort.
